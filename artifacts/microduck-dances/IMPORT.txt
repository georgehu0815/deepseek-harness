MICRODUCK DANCE IMPORTS

Open the existing DSH GUI, select a session, then open Clip & Motion Gen.
Under 3 · Animate & verify, use Load a generated dance to choose any of the six
bundled dances below. Save existing edits before switching. The dropdown loads
exact keyframes, BPM and the matching timed guide; playback stays paused and
manual review is cleared. You can edit, key, preview and save the loaded motion,
or choose the same dance again to restore its original targets.
Save existing edits and refresh the GUI once if the dropdown is not visible.

Alternatively, choose Load clip JSON and select one of these files:

bachata_microduck.clip.json       30 seconds · 130 BPM
breakdance_microduck.clip.json    30 seconds · 140 BPM
cumbia_microduck.clip.json        30 seconds · 120 BPM
martial_arts_microduck.clip.json   40 seconds · 60 BPM
robot_microduck.clip.json         40 seconds · 100 BPM
salsa_microduck.clip.json         25 seconds · 180 BPM

Use the .clip.json files with Load clip JSON, not the .sequence.json wrappers.
The timeline contains the timing; importing a bare clip does not set the recipe BPM.
The matching .sequence.json files preserve the installed model hash, joint order,
BPM and exact clip for sequence-aware consumers. The .guide.json files contain
continuous timed movement guides. manifest.json also provides each formatted guide.

Play or scrub in Simulation workspace. Review the motion, then tick
“I reviewed this revision in the preview” yourself if you want to Generate MP4.
Do not click Design sequence after importing unless you intend to replace the keys.
Only one clip is active in Animate & verify at a time. Select or import another to switch.
Dropdown loading and file import do not add browser-local Dance guide library entries.
Download or retain the six clip files; the active timeline is not durable storage.

The motions are authored interpretations, not numerical retargetings of G1 joints.
Durations and generator tempos are preserved. Unsupported arms, floor freezes,
one-leg poses and full spins are replaced with small MicroDuck movements.
Every clip starts and ends at the exact installed neutral pose with rootPitch 0.
These are not trained policies or proof of balance, contact feasibility, audio
synchronization, collision clearance or hardware safety. No physics rollout,
training or hardware activation is part of this work.
