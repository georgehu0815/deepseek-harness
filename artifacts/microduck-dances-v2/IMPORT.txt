WHAT IS LARGER
- 16–21 mm foot lifts and 22 mm forward/back swing reaches.
- Bounded whole-body headings up to about ±52°, plus larger head/neck accents.
- Feasible foot spacing, alternating support and exact neutral endpoints.
- These turn and step around the standing center; they do not force wide lateral
  splits, travel across a floor, perform floor breakdancing, strike or kick.

FORMATS AND EDITING
v2 is the dance revision in the filename, NOT JSON schema version 2.
These clips use preview schema 3 so solved root position/roll/yaw and rigid
support-foot intervals are retained. Raw pose and key editing, and training,
remain locked to protect these contacts. Renaming and retiming are available.
Retiming scales keys, contacts and the selected audio together; extreme duration
changes can exceed browser audio playback-rate support and will pause with an error.

Manual import: choose Load clip JSON and use a *.clip.json file.
A *.dance.json file is a companion containing BPM, guide and clip, not a raw import.
A *.guide.json file contains the timed descriptions.
Manual JSON import has no audio binding: use the dropdown to choose its soundtrack.

AUDIO
The sibling microduck-dance-audio folder has twelve matching *.ogg recordings:
*_microduck.ogg for originals, *_microduck_v2.ogg for the larger dances.
These are deterministic ORIGINAL genre-style instrumentals with percussion,
bass and chords. They use no commercial recordings, samples or copied melodies.
The application embeds those exact Ogg bytes and uses the selected audio clock.
No external music service is involved. Muting does not stop the shared clock.
Hidden tabs, unmounts, clip replacement and MP4 capture silence playback.
Browser playback/decode failures stop motion and show an error; retry Play sequence.
JSON and MP4 exports do NOT include the soundtrack. MP4 export remains silent.
The Ogg files are supplied separately for listening or external video editing.

EVIDENCE AND LIMITS
- manifest.json: exact clip hashes, duration/BPM, key counts and contact measurements.
- kinematic-verification.json: independent native MuJoCo forward kinematics at
  every key, contact boundary and 50 Hz; joint ranges, exact endpoints, foot
  separation, collision-foot floor clearance and support drift are measured.
- Measured support-foot drift is below 0.35 mm; the unchanged admission gate is
  0.5 mm / 0.01 rad. No kinematic threshold was loosened to accept the dances.
- browser-verification.json: exact exports, actual codec decoding, audio/motion
  clock samples and shared playback controls for the twelve dropdown choices.
  Headless rendering cadence is reported separately from clock agreement.
- *-step-audio.png and *-turn-audio.png: live GUI views of the six larger dances.
- The audio manifest records file hashes, duration/BPM and composition provenance.
These are AUTHORED KINEMATIC PREVIEWS, not learned skills, physics simulation,
balance assessment or proof of hardware safety. No training, dynamics stepping
or hardware activation was performed. Sampled checks are not continuous proofs.
